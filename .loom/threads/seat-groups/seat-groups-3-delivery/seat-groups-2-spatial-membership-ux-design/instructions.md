# seat-groups-2-spatial-membership-ux-design

Design how the Seats workspace reveals the spatial distribution of groups
before the group UI is implemented.

Bob's required outcome: selecting or inspecting a group should highlight its
Seats on the spatial map so its distribution is understandable at a glance.
Explore simultaneous display of multiple groups with multiple colours and give
Seats with overlapping memberships an explicit, legible treatment.

Obtain a dedicated UX design/review pass and produce a written proposal with
small visual studies or browser-native prototypes as useful. Evaluate at least:

- one selected group versus several simultaneously visible groups;
- overlapping memberships without misleading blended colours;
- legend, filtering, focus, hover/selection, and clear-all behavior;
- distinguishability for colour-vision deficiencies and in low-contrast rooms;
- map clutter at installation scale and touch/iPad interaction;
- whether colour is group-authored, automatically assigned, or view-local;
- how empty, hidden, deleted, and renamed groups behave;
- consistency between group authoring and live-control target selection.

This is a user-facing design gate. Lore-keep the alternatives and recommendation,
mark the stitch waiting, and request Bob's ratification before UI implementation.
Do not change production UI or the ratified OSC/data model in this stitch.
