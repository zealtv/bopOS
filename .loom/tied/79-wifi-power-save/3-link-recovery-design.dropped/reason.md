# why 3-link-recovery-design was dropped

With power save off, NetworkManager's own reconnect brought noor-dusk back unaided after 11/11 sixty-second AP restarts (2026-11-05, lore:2026-11-05-ap-restart-soak-noor-dusk). That was the condition this stitch named for a bopOS nudge being unnecessary. Limits: one node, strong signal, 60 s outages. The one case where a nudge might still earn its place, a possible wrong-key lockout, now lives in 33b-device-network-config/3-wrong-key-lockout.
