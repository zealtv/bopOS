# Engine-boundary ratification

Ratification session with Bob, 2026-07-12.

## Architectural direction

**Accepted.** Adopt the council's sole-binder end state: `bopos.py` is the
only LAN citizen on each device, and every engine, including Pure Data,
consumes the same selector-stripped localhost surface on port 6661.

Bob's reason: this is a good design move.

The acceptance includes the council's ordering discipline: retain PD's direct
6660 path until the parallel relay is proven, require the helper-death drill
and production-Mac N=1 bench gate before removing that path, and stop for
reconsideration if recovery is not within seconds.

## Engine-facing contract

**Accepted as proposed.** All engines receive this selector-stripped OSC
surface on localhost 6661:

```
/id <n>
/os/master <0..1>
/p/<name> <values...>
/pt <point> <element> <value>
/cue <id>
/notify <event>
```

The engine-to-framework surface on localhost 7770 is request-only:

```
/config
/store
/load
/report <name> <values...>
```

Administrative commands do not cross from an engine to the framework;
`bopos.py` handles them directly from the LAN contract.

## Naming

**Accepted as proposed.** After behavioral migration, rename `helper.py` to
`bopos.py` in a separate behavior-free change. In Bob's PD edit wave, rename
`pd/bopos.osc.pd` to `pd/bopos.pd` and expose it as `[bopos]`.

The PD bus glossary is:

- incoming: `bopos-master`, `bopos-param`, `bopos-point`, `bopos-cue`,
  `bopos-notify`, `bopos-io`, `bopos-context`;
- outgoing: `to-bopos-io`, `to-bopos-report`.

The patch-facing `osc-in`, `osc-out`, `ID`, `PX`, `PY`, `route-by-id`, and
in-patch selector/identity routing are retired.

## Identity, run context, and time

**Accepted with an amendment.** `bopos.py` owns identity and run context.
Identity remains OSC: `/id` is pushed on assignment and answered after an
engine's retrying `/config` request. Run context (seed, run id string, patch,
assets) is generated by a bopOS-owned step and delivered atomically at launch
through `start-engine.sh` (`-send` for PD or environment for other engines).
Standalone launch degrades to a useful standalone context rather than silence.

The council's statement that absolute wall time must never enter an engine was
**rejected as overly broad**. The amended constraint is:

> Absolute wall-clock timestamps must not be sent as OSC floats or used by
> engines for synchronized cue timing. bopOS may provide civil date/time to an
> engine, safely encoded, for patch-level calendar behaviour.

Synchronized cue scheduling remains on Python's monotonic clock; engines
receive the relative `/cue <id>` fire. Future civil-time access is permitted,
including uses such as calendar-time events, but its request/event interface
and implementation are deliberately deferred.

## Compatibility and rewrite policy

**Accepted with a clean-break amendment.** There are no deployed dashboards;
the dashboard exists only in the current development stream. Therefore no
`/helper/*` compatibility alias will be added. The dashboard moves directly
to `/os/*`, and obsolete consumers are not preserved speculatively.

PD's direct 6660 path remains only as a temporary verification safety net
while the common 6661 relay is proved. After the helper-death and production-
Mac N=1 bench gates pass, patches rewrite in lockstep to `[bopos]` and the
`bopos-*` glossary. The old 7770 admin forwarding, `/rpt` echo chain,
`osc-in`/`osc-out`, direct PD LAN binding, and in-patch identity routing are
removed rather than maintained in parallel.

Contract v1.2 explicitly supersedes v1.1's statement that PD retains its
direct 6660 route. The shared OSC client's send lock remains required as a
real concurrency fix, independent of compatibility.

## Reports, probes, and meters

**Accepted with deferral and deletion amendments.** Delete framework meter
streaming (`meter_loop`, `METERS`, and `METER_INTERVAL`), the legacy broadcast
`/rpt` and echo chain, and manifest `role: "meter"` entirely. Patch reports do
not acquire presentation metadata in v1.2; a future reporting UI should design
explicit read-only presentation semantics from concrete needs.

Add the demand-driven one-shot probe:

```
/<id>/os/probe <what>
  -> /os/probe <id> <what> <values...>
```

It answers from values already held by `bopos.py`, including patch-authored
values received through `/report <name> <values...>`.

The leased probe is **left open and is not part of contract v1.2**. Preserve
the council proposal as reference material rather than a ratified wire shape:
`/<id>/os/probe <what> <hz> <ttl>`, auto-expiring, proposed caps of 10 Hz and
60 seconds, and an io-to-`bopos.py` upstream required for touch debugging. A
future concrete need must drive its design and ratification.

## IO boundary

**Accepted as proposed.** Retain io-to-engine streams on localhost 6662 and
engine-to-io control on localhost 8880. Do not fold peripheral traffic into
the 6661 engine-control surface. `[bopos]` presents these through `bopos-io`
and `to-bopos-io` while preserving transport isolation from potentially
blocking I2C work and higher-rate peripheral bundles.

Deprecate io's overlapping `/system/*` facts in favour of framework-owned
`/os/report`, but prune them later because they are not load-bearing.

## Superseded tied behaviour

**Confirmed.** Contract v1.2 explicitly supersedes:

- contract v1.1 §4/§4.1's statement that PD retains its direct 6660 path;
- `seam-5`'s non-PD-only relay condition (the relay becomes engine-agnostic);
- `audition-1b`'s fixed-port bind attempts and expected macOS collision noise;
- dashboard framework-meter streaming and manifest `role: "meter"`;
- PD's legacy `/rpt`, `route echo`, admin-forwarding, and transport-aware
  identity routing, including tied behaviour that relied upon them.

The council's proposed `/helper/*` compatibility alias and leased-probe wire
shape are not adopted. The v1.1 seam law remains intact: bopOS provides terms
but does not compose them into patch parameters, and only mute is framework-
enforced.

## Ordered implementation stitches

**Accepted.** Implement in this dependency order:

1. `boundary-1-client-lock` — lock the shared engine OSC client and soak-test
   simultaneous cue and LAN delivery. No compatibility alias.
2. `boundary-2-pd-parallel-relay` — relay master and `/p/*` to PD on 6661 in
   parallel with its retained 6660 path; verify the common surface first.
3. `boundary-3-framework-slimdown` — remove 7770 admin handlers, framework
   meter streaming/configuration, and `role: "meter"` across manifest and
   dashboard; retain `/config`, `/store`, `/load`; add `/report` and the
   one-shot probe; run the helper-death drill and require restoration of
   control within seconds.
4. `boundary-4-pd-edit-wave` — agent writes the exact spec in
   `.notes/pd-edits-for-bob.md`; Bob edits the `.pd` files. Remove the 6660
   listener, admin forward, echo/report chain, internal selector/identity
   routing, and legacy feedback; introduce `[bopos]`, the ratified buses, and
   configurable ingress; verify a production-style single PD engine on macOS.
5. `boundary-5-launch-context-and-topology` — move run-context ownership into
   bopOS while retaining launch-time delivery; add SC `/config` retry; remove
   audition fixed-bind attempts; unify relay implementation/library where
   practical.
6. `boundary-6-contract-v12-and-renames` — update the durable contract with
   all ratified decisions and supersessions; rename `helper.py` to `bopos.py`
   as a separate behavior-free final change. The PD abstraction rename belongs
   to Bob's edit wave.

Any failed safety gate stops the sequence before PD's direct path is removed.

## Deliberately open decisions

- Civil-time access is permitted in principle; encoding and request/event API
  are deferred.
- A possible generic bopOS-owned capability-provider/plugin interface remains
  open as a future modularity direction. Python adapters could bridge civil
  time, external APIs, or other capabilities an engine does not provide. This
  note does not ratify an architecture or add it to contract v1.2.
- The leased-probe council design is reference material only; its wire shape,
  limits, and implementation are unratified.
- Patch-report presentation has no `role: "meter"` replacement yet.
- The helper-death threshold (control restored "within seconds") must be
  measured. Failure reopens the sole-binder risk before PD's direct route is
  removed.
- Production macOS N=1 verification is required before removing PD's 6660
  bind, and full-seam verification requires a real rig.
- io `/system/*` is deprecated, but deletion timing is deferred.
- Exact relay-library unification remains an implementation detail to decide
  from migration evidence.

## Outcome

**Ratified with the amendments recorded above.** Proceed through the ordered
implementation chain; do not implement deferred/open designs as if ratified.
