# Some choices are Bob's to ratify

When a stitch reaches a user-facing, wire, or strategy decision, write a proposal and stop rather than implementing past it.

Bob ratifies: OSC wire and port changes, scene-language syntax, engine strategy, and anything user-facing in the Remote (facilitator/iPad) view or other operator-visible UI naming. Produce the proposal in the stitch (or keep it in lore), mark the stitch `.waiting`, and surface it. Prefer mockups generated from the running app over drawn ones.

A ruling covers the question it answered. "Do whatever is cleanest" (or any
latitude Bob grants) is spot advice for that item, not a standing ruling:
contract text, wire changes and operator-visible wording that follow from it
still come back as proposals (Bob, 2026-10-03).
