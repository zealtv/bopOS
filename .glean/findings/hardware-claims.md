# Don't claim hardware you didn't run

When a stitch involves real Pis, audio or peripherals, record which half ran — software gates and hardware checks are separate claims.

simfleet, Playwright's `pointer:coarse`, and loopback sync numbers don't prove rig behaviour. Write the split into the stitch's `verification.md` (the `44/5-pd-adoption` record is the model). Rig access and its limits (no flashing, reimaging or `apt upgrade` without asking; sudo needs Bob) are in [[test-rig]].
