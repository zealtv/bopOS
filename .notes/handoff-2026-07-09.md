## Gotchas (healed where noted)

- **Multiple real helper.py on one host is impossible** — fixed ports (7770,
  6660). simfleet is the multi-device sim vehicle; sync-2 proved one real
  helper.py on loopback. Parameterising helper's ports would enable a richer
  all-software audible/real mix — a possible small follow-up stitch (not
  required for clock-sync). *Not healed* (it's new work, not a doc error.)
- **Claim before writing artifacts** — writing to a guessed `<stitch>.stitching/`
  path before `claim` auto-creates a phantom dir and the later claim errors
  "multiple stitches found". Hit it on sync-2; recovered (moved artifacts,
  rmdir, claimed). *Healed* in the loom-autopilot skill Learnings.
- **On Linux `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` is system-wide**, so the dashboard/measure-tool
  leader and simfleet/helper share a clock — a correct offset ≈ the injected
  skew. Every sync verify exploits this to check convergence with no hardware.
- **Verifies are browser-free** (sync plane is LAN/engine only) but need deps:
  `python-osc` (sync-0/3), `+ websockets fastapi uvicorn` (sync-1),
  `+ pyOSC3` (sync-2). Documented per stitch. The three **Playwright** dashboard
  suites were **not** re-run this session (no chromium here); the changes are
  server-side/additive with no `static/` edits, and each sync verify carries
  ws-level regression — see `sync-1`'s results caveat.
